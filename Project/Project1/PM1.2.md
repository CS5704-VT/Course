This project milestone will focus on the design of your system.

### Low-level Design (4%)

Provide a brief rationale (no more than 1 paragraph) explaining some of the design decisions for your program based on the provided sketch. Also discuss which of the usability heuristics played a role in the UI design of your system. Which ones might your proposed design lack?
