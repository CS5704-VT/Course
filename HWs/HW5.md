## The Impact of AI-Assisted Tools on Software Engineering 

Artificial intelligence (AI) tools are reshaping how software is written, reviewed, tested, and maintained. Yet, the empirical evidence for their actual impact on productivity, code quality, and software development remains inconclusive. Early studies and industry sources suggest AI tools significantly enhance productivity. Hoowever, recent studies suggest increases in speed may come at a cost to code quality[^1] and developers' abilities[^2]. 

For this assignment, you will design a research study to investigate one or more impacts of AI tools on software engineering practice. You will not conduct the study, but will devise a plan for an investigation that you would be potentially interested in carrying out. Your plan should meet the following criteria:

**Section 1: Research Question(s) and Motivation.** Provide **at least one** research question (RQ) that your study will investigate. Insights on creating a research question are available here. In addition, write a brief motivation explaining why the question(s) is/are relevant to software engineering in practice. 

**Section 2: Study Context.** Describe the specific context in which your study would take place. Potential topics may include:

- **AI tool(s) under study:** Which specific tool(s) will you study and why? 
- **Environment type:** What type of setting will you study the tool(s) in and why?
- **Study context:** Define who or what you are studying. For instance, if your study involves human participants---who are the participants? If your study involves software repositories---which projects?
- etc.

**Section 3: Data Collection.** Describe what **data** you will collect and how with regard to your RQ. You should also define the specific **metrics** you will use to measure your RQ(s). For instance, how would you measure "productivity" or "quality"?

**Section 4: Analysis Strategy and Validity.** Briefly Describe how you will analyze the data to answer your RQ. In addition, please note any limitations your study design would face.
